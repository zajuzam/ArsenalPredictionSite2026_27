About This Site

Arsenal Predictor — 2026/27 Season

My Passion for Arsenal

I am a lifelong and passionate Arsenal fan, and this website was created purely out of my love for the club and the beautiful game of football.

My journey as an Arsenal supporter began when Dennis Bergkamp joined the club. As a huge supporter of the Netherlands national football team, I was already a big admirer of Bergkamp's incredible talent, intelligence, and elegant style of play. When he became an Arsenal player, I naturally started following the club—and over the years, Arsenal became a huge part of my life.

Since then, I have followed Arsenal through the highs and lows: the unforgettable victories, the difficult seasons, the legendary players, and the moments that have made being a Gooner such a special experience. Arsenal is more than just a football club to me—it is a passion, a tradition, and a community shared by millions of fans around the world.

One of the happiest moments of my journey as an Arsenal supporter came in the 2025/26 season, when Arsenal finally won the Premier League title after a 22-year wait. Seeing Arsenal become champions again was an incredibly emotional and unforgettable moment for me and for Gooners everywhere.

I created this website to celebrate Arsenal Football Club, its history, its players, its achievements, and the passion of its supporters. This is my personal tribute to the club I love.

Come on you Gunners!

How to Play

Predict the scores. Bank the points. Top the table.

1. Join

Create an account with a username, email and a 4-digit PIN. Your PIN is how you log back in, so keep it safe — an admin can reset it if you ever forget.

2. Predict the score

For each of Arsenal's 38 league games, enter your predicted final score and hit Predict. You can change a prediction as many times as you like until it locks.

3. Deadline

Predictions lock 30 minutes before kick-off. Once locked, the card greys out and can no longer be changed — the countdown timer on each card shows how long you have left.

4. Scoring

Exact score = 3 points. Correct result (win, draw or loss) with the wrong score = 1 point. Anything else = 0.

5. Leaderboard

Points build up across the season. The Leaderboard shows your rank, your country flag, and how many exact scores and correct results you've landed.

🏆 The Prize

The player who finishes top of the leaderboard at the end of the season wins an Original Arsenal Home Shirt. Predict well all season long for your shot at the prize!

Tips

Every card shows the stadium, both team captains, and how all the other players predicted the match — use the crowd split to guide (or defy) your pick.

Privacy Policy

How Arsenal Predictor handles your data

Last updated: 26 July 2026

Who we are

Arsenal Predictor is an unofficial, fan-run football prediction game operated by Pravda Software LLC ("we", "us"). We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or associated with Arsenal Football Club or the Premier League. You can reach us at pravdasoftware@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you create an account we store your chosen username, your email address, and a securely hashed version of your 4-digit PIN (we never store the PIN itself). We also store the score predictions you make. To show a country flag on the leaderboard, we make a best-effort lookup of your approximate country and IP address when you sign up.

How we use it

Your data is used only to run the game: to sign you in, save and score your predictions, and display the leaderboard. We do not sell your data, and we do not send you marketing emails unless you have specifically asked us to.

Where it is stored

Account and prediction data is stored in our database hosted by Supabase. Some page content (club crests, country flags, web fonts) is loaded from third-party services such as ESPN, flagcdn and Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address as part of delivering those files.

Cookies and local storage

We use your browser's local storage only for essential functions — keeping you signed in and remembering your light/dark theme choice. We do not currently use advertising or tracking cookies. If that ever changes, we will update this policy and ask for your consent first.

Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you at any time by emailing pravdasoftware@gmail.com. If you are in the UK or EU, you also have rights under the UK GDPR / GDPR, including the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

Children

This site is intended for users aged 13 and over. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will remove it.
